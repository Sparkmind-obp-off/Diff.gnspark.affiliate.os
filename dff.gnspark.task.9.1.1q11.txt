5. TASK 09 SCOPE

Implement the foundational demand discovery capability covering:

A. Demand Signal Domain

A demand signal represents evidence that a real problem, need, behavior, interest, or commercial intent exists.

The signal should support concepts such as:

search
conversation
content
commercial intent
transaction
creator
trend

Use deterministic enums/types rather than arbitrary uncontrolled strings where appropriate.

7. EVIDENCE-FIRST PRINCIPLE

Demand Discovery MUST be evidence-first.

A demand signal cannot be considered strong merely because:

an AI model predicted it,
a keyword sounds attractive,
a trend appears interesting,
a creator has many followers,
or a hypothetical market assumption was made.

The system must preserve evidence provenance.

At minimum support:

source_type
source_ref
observed_at
evidence
confidence

The architecture must make it possible to answer:

"Why does Affiliate OS believe this demand exists?"

9. DEMAND NORMALIZATION

Implement deterministic normalization for the core problem representation.

"  Susah Cari Produk Skincare Untuk Kulit Sensitif! "

surrounding whitespace

10. DEMAND SCORING

Implement a deterministic foundational demand score.

The score must:

be bounded
be reproducible
use explicit rules
account for signal strength
account for confidence
account for signal type where appropriate

Do NOT introduce an opaque AI-generated score.

The scoring model must be understandable and testable.

Do not pretend this is the final production intelligence model.

This is the foundation.

11. CONFIDENCE

Support explicit confidence levels, for example:

low
medium
high
very_high

Confidence must remain separate from raw signal value.

The system should distinguish:

"The observed signal is strong"

from:

"We are highly confident the evidence is reliable."

12. DEMAND STATUS

Create a controlled lifecycle for demand signals.

A foundational status model may include:

DISCOVERED
OBSERVING
VALIDATING
CONFIRMED
OPPORTUNITY_READY

Do not create arbitrary/custom states.

The status should communicate the maturity of the evidence.

Do NOT automatically create an Opportunity merely because a signal reaches OPPORTUNITY_READY.

Opportunity creation remains a separate controlled concern.

13. DUPLICATE / FINGERPRINT PROTECTION

Demand signals must have deterministic duplicate protection.

Use a stable fingerprint derived from relevant normalized attributes.

The fingerprint must be tenant-scoped.

The database must enforce uniqueness.

A duplicate submission must produce a safe deterministic conflict/result.

Do NOT rely solely on application-memory deduplication.

14. TENANCY

Every persisted demand signal MUST belong to a workspace.

The database must enforce:

workspace_id

and the repository must always query using tenant scope.

Examples:

findById(workspaceId, id)
list(workspaceId, ...)

Never expose:

findById(id)

for tenant-owned demand resources unless tenant authorization is already guaranteed by a higher-level abstraction and repository design explicitly preserves the boundary.

Defense in depth is required.

15. AUTHORIZATION

Do NOT create a second RBAC system.

demand.create

Authorization must happen before resource access.

Deny by default.

Cross-workspace access must be impossible.
